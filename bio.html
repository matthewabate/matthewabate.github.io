Bio:

I work as an experienced strategist focusing on how people make decisions in the real world, the contexts, systems, and constraints that dictate behavior, and how companies can use that knowledge to solve problems.

Most of my work involves situations where teams aren’t sure they understand the problem, or where observations contradict data. I help them turn ambiguity into clarity and discard wrong assumptions, ensuring the problem they’re solving is the one they should go after.
