Making complex gameplay easier through vibe coding.

Irminsul  ·  Personal Project  ·  2024–Ongoing

The Challenge

My kids and their friends love Genshin Impact, a fantasy open world action RPG they can play on a console, mobile phone, or PC with around 5 million active players per day. It has immersive stories, great anime-style cut scenes, and rich lore that keeps them in it no matter how much work it takes.

But it is genuinely too complicated. Between 113 playable characters, thousands of equipment combinations, cooperative and team-based play that rewards deep optimization, they struggle to keep up with everything and it becomes overwhelming.

The community has built tools to help, but they fall short, requiring real expertise to interpret, or being so generic they aren’t relevant to any specific player’s inventory. Neither approach answers the question a player is actually asking: “Given what I have, what should I do?”

I'd been watching this problem for a while and noticed exactly the kind of gap I see at work. My kids have an unmet need with requirements and plenty of data to inform decisions. So I decided to build the tool myself.

What I Did

Interviewed my kids to understand what they were really trying to accomplish and what about the game was making hard to manage. I watched them play and noticed how they differed in their approaches. This helped me understand how much they wanted to understand vs how much they wanted decisions made for them, and at what point they lost interest.

I compared the problems that needed to be solved to the resources already available out in the world, made an inventory of relevant data sources, and worked out a strategy for compiling high-quality recommendations from publicly available information .

From there I built a prototype mobile app that generates conflict-free team recommendations with plain-language explanations of the reasoning behind them. It compares a player's actual account configurations to online discussions of character and team builds, weapon and equipment pairings, and variations in team performance in different scenarios. Data is collected via the APIs provided by the game manufacturer, fan community and social media sites, and discussion platforms like Reddit, Discord.

The product architecture, data sourcing, logic, testing and refinement we made using Claude to write code, which meant I could move faster, iterate in real time, and maintain full ownership of the direction.

This project serves as an illustration of how I think about AI as a tool in design and strategy work. Using AI as a capable partner, I was able to identify a real problem in a complex domain, plan and manage a complex project beyond my personal skillset, and develop a product from nothing to a live Beta without losing the thread of what the experience should actually do for a person.

Status

Live and in closed testing. Early feedback validated the core concept (users prefer proactive guidance and recommendations to searching through online resources). Active development continues toward integrating AI to execute a content strategy, with plans for a final design in the App Store some time in 2026.
